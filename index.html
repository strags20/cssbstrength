How many workouts are there per week?

Three structured workouts per week with an optional 4th session.

Do I need a gym?

Nope! Both gym and dumbbell-only workouts are included. You'll need at least one lighter and one heavier set of dumbbells.

Can I pay monthly?

We ask for a 3-month minimum commitment to ensure results. One month isn't nearly enough time and we want to set you up for success.

How long until I see results?

You'll feel stronger within 2-3 weeks. Visible changes usually appear around week 8-12.
