FAQ

Is this program for beginners?

This program is best for women who already have some weightlifting experience. But I promise to personally help you start where you are. The program meets you at your level—whether you've been lifting regularly or you're getting back into it. You don't need to be "ready." You just need to start.

How many workouts are there per week?

The program includes 3 structured workouts per week with an optional 4th workout. Three sessions per week are enough for significant results, and you can add the fourth to accelerate progress.

Do I need a gym?

Nope! There are both gym and dumbbell-only workouts included. You'll need at least one lighter and one heavier set of dumbbells to get the most out of the program.

Can I pay monthly?

We ask for a 3-month minimum commitment so you see results. One month isn't nearly enough time and we want to set you up for success.

How long until I see results?

You'll feel stronger within 2-3 weeks. Visible changes typically appear around week 8-12.

What if I have restrictions?

If you have injuries or limitations, email us at info@christenstrags.com to discuss more personalized plan options.
