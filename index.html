FAQ

Is this program for beginners?

This program is best for women who already have some weightlifting experience. But I promise to personally help you start where you are. The program meets you at your level—whether you've been lifting regularly or you're getting back into it.

How many workouts per week?

Three structured workouts per week (30-45 minutes each) with an optional 4th session. It's designed for real schedules and if you miss a workout or two, you can just pick right back up where you left off.

Do I need a gym?

Nope! You get access to both gym and dumbbell-only workouts. You'll need at least one lighter and one heavier set of dumbbells for the DB-only version. Both programs are loaded into your app so you can do either one or swap back and forth when you need to.

Can I pay monthly?

We offer a 3 month membership and an annual membership. Real strength takes time to build and you need more than one month to see results.

How long until I see results?

You'll feel stronger within 2-3 weeks. Visible changes usually appear around week 8-12.
