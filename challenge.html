How It Works

Simple as the
Tournament Itself

01

Pick Your Team

Choose one team from the full tournament field. That's your team for the entire challenge. You ride with them — all the way or until they're out.

02

Train When They Win

Every time your team wins, you do the Survive & Advance workout for that round. Progressive programming, dumbbells and bodyweight only. No gym required.

03

Go as Far as They Go

If your team wins the championship, you complete all 6 workouts and finish strong with them. If they get knocked out, you do one final Send-Off workout and finish strong.
