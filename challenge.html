Questions

FAQ

Do I need a gym?

No. Every workout has a gym version and a dumbbell-only version. All you need is a set of dumbbells and 30–45 minutes.

Do I need to be an SBS member?

No. The challenge is open to everyone at $49. SBS members get in for $20.

How does the scoring work?

10 points per workout completed. 5 points per correct Final Four team (max 4). 10 points per correct championship team (max 2). 15 points for correct champion. Highest total wins.

When do picks lock?

11:59pm CST on Tuesday March 17th. Submit your form when you sign up. No changes after the deadline.

What if I miss a workout?

You miss those 10 points. Show up as much as you can — every workout moves you up the leaderboard.

What is the Championship Day watch party?

A virtual celebration on April 7th for all challenge participants. We watch the championship game together, celebrate everyone who finished, and announce the $200 winner live.

How do I enter for free?

Email info@christenstrags.com with "March Championship Run Entry" in the subject line before March 17, 2026. Open to US residents 18 and older.
